🐾 Our Caregivers

At Trusted Paws, our caregivers are the heart of everything we do. They are dedicated animal care professionals responsible for the wellbeing, development, and emotional support of every dog in our adoption program.

Unlike traditional pet service providers, we do not offer daycare, boarding, or training services for privately owned dogs. Our caregivers focus exclusively on dogs within our care.

🐶 What Our Caregivers Do

📋 Daily Care & Monitoring

  • Feeding, grooming, hygiene
  • Continuous health observation

🧠 Behavioral Support

  • Building confidence
  • Positive behaviors & socialization

🩺 Health & Wellness

  • Working with veterinarians
  • Vaccinations & check-ups

❤️ Emotional Care

  • Attention, affection, stable routine
  • Mental wellbeing support

🏡 Adoption Readiness

  • Temperament assessment
  • Matching with right family

❤️ Our Commitment to Ethical Care

  • Safe, clean, comfortable living conditions
  • Humane treatment & positive reinforcement
  • Individualized attention for every dog
  • Transparency throughout adoption process
🐕 Our mission is solely focused on rescue, rehabilitation, and responsible adoption.

🌟 Why It Matters

The role of our caregivers goes beyond basic care—they help transform lives. Through patience, dedication, and compassion, they prepare each dog not just to be adopted, but to thrive in a new home.

🚚 Dog Delivery Services (Company Division)

The Delivery Service is a specialized division of our company responsible for the safe, reliable, and stress-free transportation of dogs to and from designated locations.

📌 Scope of Delivery Services

🏠 Home Delivery
🔄 Pick-Up Services
🌍 Inter-City & Long-Distance
⚡ Scheduled & On-Demand

🐾 Handling & Transportation Standards

  • Well-ventilated, clean, secure transport vehicles
  • Comfortable crates or carriers suited to dog's size
  • Regular monitoring during transit
  • Minimal travel stress through proper handling

🛡️ Safety & Compliance

  • Pre-transport health checks and documentation
  • Trained handlers experienced in animal transportation
  • Adherence to local pet transport regulations
  • Emergency preparedness during transit

⏰ Client Convenience

  • Saving time and reducing travel stress for clients
  • Door-to-door service
  • Real-time updates during delivery
